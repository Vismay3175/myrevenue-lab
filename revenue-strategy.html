Analytical Excellence

How Data Outperforms Competitor Rate Sets

At My Revenue Lab, our methodology centers on unconstrained demand forecasting and real-time market rate adjustments. Rather than relying on static seasonal tables, our team evaluates over 10,000 daily market data points to continuously balance occupancy velocity against Average Daily Rate (ADR).

By integrating automated Revenue Management Systems (RMS), direct channel booking incentives, and OTA parity monitoring, we ensure hotel properties maximize Gross Operating Profit (GOP) and bank deposit returns.

Commercial Pillars

Our 5 Core Yield Modules

Data-driven yield engineering frameworks designed to outpace competitive rate sets and drive net RevPAR gains.

1. Dynamic Rate Optimization

Automated price fluctuations calculated every hour based on pick-up pace, competitor pricing, and length-of-stay restrictions.

2. Channel Distribution Control

Full synchronization across Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, GDS, and direct engines to prevent rate leaks and penalty commissions.

3. Market Share Capture

Strategic positioning relative to competitive sets to expand RevPAR Index (RGI) above 100% fair market share.

4. Direct Booking Conversion Engine

Website optimization, rate parity alignment, and direct guest loyalty perks to reduce overall OTA acquisition cost.

5. Group & Corporate Contracting

Displacement analysis to evaluate corporate tenders, sports teams, and MICE groups against transient revenue replacement.
