Hotel Yield & Revenue Glossary

Master every key acronym, formula, and strategic metric used by top hotel revenue managers worldwide.

ADR (Average Daily Rate)

Core Metric
Formula: Total Room Revenue / Paid Rooms Sold

The average rental income generated per occupied room on a given day. ADR measures room pricing strength excluding complimentary rooms and unallocated food & beverage revenue.

Example: If your hotel generates $20,000 from 100 occupied rooms, your ADR is $200.00.

ARI (Average Rate Index)

Comp Set Index
Formula: (Your Hotel ADR / Comp Set Average ADR) * 100

Measures your hotel’s ADR performance relative to your competitive set. An ARI above 100 indicates your property commands a premium rate compared to your competitors.

BAR (Best Available Rate)

Pricing Term

The lowest non-restricted rate available to the general public for a specific room type on a given date. Serves as the anchor benchmark from which discounted (corporate/group) or premium rates are derived.

Booking Window (Lead Time)

Demand Pattern

The timeframe between when a guest makes a reservation and their actual check-in date. Analyzing lead time curves prevents premature rate discounting during prime booking windows.

Channel Mix

Distribution

The percentage breakdown of bookings originating from various distribution channels (Brand Direct, OTAs like Booking/Expedia, Wholesalers, GDS, and Phone).

Comp Set (Competitive Set)

Benchmarking

A curated group of 4 to 6 local peer hotels that compete directly for the same target guests based on location, star rating, amenities, price tier, and room count.

CTA / CTD (Closed to Arrival / Departure)

Restriction

Yield management restrictions that block check-ins (CTA) or check-outs (CTD) on high-demand peak event dates to protect multi-night stays and eliminate shoulder-date vacancy.

Displacement Analysis

Strategy Tool

A mathematical calculation comparing the total profit potential of accepting a low-rate group booking versus rejecting it to sell higher-ADR transient rooms to individual guests.

Fenced Rates (Rate Fences)

Pricing Tool

Specific conditions (non-refundable prepay, 14-day advance purchase, loyalty membership) attached to discounted rates to prevent high-paying business travelers from booking discounted leisure rates.

MPI (Market Penetration Index)

Comp Set Index
Formula: (Your Hotel Occupancy / Comp Set Average Occupancy) * 100

Measures whether your property is capturing its fair share of total market room night demand compared to your competitors.

Net RevPAR (NRevPAR)

Profit Metric
Formula: (Room Revenue - Distribution Costs) / Total Available Rooms

Similar to standard RevPAR, but deducts OTA commissions, booking transaction fees, and distribution expenses to reveal true net profit per available key.

Occupancy (OCC)

Core Metric
Formula: (Rooms Sold / Total Available Rooms) * 100

The percentage of total available rooms filled over a given night or date range.

Rate Parity

Distribution

Maintaining consistent public rates across all online channels (Direct Website, Booking.com, Expedia, Google Hotel Ads). Prevents OTA penalties while leveraging member rates for direct conversion.

RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)

Gold Standard
Formula: ADR * Occupancy % OR Total Room Revenue / Total Available Rooms

The single most important performance metric in hotel revenue management, balancing both occupancy percentage and price rate simultaneously.

RGI (Revenue Generator Index / RevPAR Index)

Comp Set Gold
Formula: (Your Hotel RevPAR / Comp Set Average RevPAR) * 100

Evaluates your hotel’s overall financial yield relative to its comp set. An RGI above 100 proves your property is outperforming its fair market share.

Unconstrained Demand

Yield Science

The total room demand that could be sold if your hotel had unlimited physical room capacity. Identifying unconstrained demand during major events allows aggressive rate hikes.
